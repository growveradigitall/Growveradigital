How this website handles your project enquiry.

Contact enquiries

The contact form prepares an email draft in your own email application. Form details are not submitted to a website server or stored by this website. The draft is only sent if you choose to send it in your email app.

Information you share

If you email Growvera Digital, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your name, contact details and project requirements. We use that information to respond and discuss your enquiry.

Cookies and analytics

This version of the website does not install analytics or marketing cookies. Your hosting provider may process technical request information to deliver the website.

Questions
