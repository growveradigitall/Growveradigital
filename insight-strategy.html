STRATEGY

Start with a goal, not a platform.

A practical starting point for choosing your digital marketing channels.

← All insights

Before choosing Instagram, Google Ads or SEO, get clear on the business outcome you want. Is the priority local enquiries, brand awareness or online sales? Each goal asks for a different approach.

Next, consider where your audience looks for answers. Search can meet an existing need, while social content can build familiarity over time. Your website should make the next step clear in either case.

Choose a manageable starting scope. Define what you will measure, establish a baseline and review what you learn before adding more channels.
