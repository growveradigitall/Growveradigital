WEB DESIGN

A website should make the next step easy.

Three things to prioritise before adding another animation.

← All insights

A clear message comes first. Visitors should quickly understand what your business offers, who it helps and why it is relevant to them.

Make key information easy to find on a phone. Use readable text, purposeful navigation and clear contact actions. Large effects should never get in the way of using the page.

Give each page a helpful next step. A service page might lead to a focused enquiry, while an article can point readers to a relevant service. Test the journey from start to finish.

Start with your business.
